# Recruitment manager MVP acceptance plan

Goal: reliable local recruitment content, monitoring and evidence-led planning on the current NVIDIA T1200 4 GB system. No paid providers or larger models assumed. Existing posts are immutable unless the operator explicitly edits them.

## 1. Baseline and regression gates (in progress)

- Full backend suite, frontend tests and production build must pass without exclusions.
- Test requirements reflect safe new content rather than copying obsolete behavior.
- Verify actual health and local model responses, not just mock tests.

## 2. New content correctness (in progress)

- All rate variants retain their categories, units and eligibility; no housing, phone, age or schedule contamination.
- No duplicated opening, Telegram CTA, mixed language, invented facts or silent truncation.
- Invalid/ambiguous output remains a review draft; never auto-publish a fallback or uncertain source.
- Test realistic multilingual sources and an actual local inference end to end.

## 3. Monitoring and analysis (in progress)

- Own-account metrics distinguish missing data from zero; latest snapshots do not double-count.
- Public search permission errors are actionable, do not break own-account collection, and recover when access is restored.
- Public sources are bounded, deduplicated and dated. News is not falsely labelled Threads evidence.
- Dashboard and analyst share evidence; compare compatible language and age cohorts only.

## 4. Adaptive editorial loop (in progress)

- Planner receives recent topics, real metrics, limitations and hypotheses.
- Persist feedback from operator review and evaluated experiments; avoid unsupported claims of model training.
- New plans demonstrate use of feedback and anti-repetition tests; no changes to historical posts.

## 5. Operational stability and delivery (pending)

- Bounded retries/timeouts, idempotent external operations, crash recovery, sanitized errors and backup/restore instructions.
- CPU/GPU/RAM budgets remain compatible with hardware; no simultaneous heavy generation overload.
- Verify UI/API/job lifecycle and restart recovery; document external permission blockers precisely.
- Image/video workflows and three aspect ratios are verified where configured; mock output is never presented as real generation.
- MVP cannot be declared finished on green health alone or before each requirement has evidence.

Initial audit: 108 backend tests passed, 5 failed. Public Threads search reports auth; own-account metrics exist. Hardware: NVIDIA T1200 Laptop GPU, 4096 MiB. Absolute zero failures cannot be promised for external services; acceptance is bounded recovery and clear degraded states.
